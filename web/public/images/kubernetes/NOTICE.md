# Kubernetes icons

The SVG files in this folder are the unlabeled icons of the Kubernetes icon set, from
[kubernetes/community](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/tree/307ae50e8e4246de172f195c73e3f026b4f0c7ff/icons)
(`icons/svg/resources/unlabeled/` and `icons/svg/infrastructure_components/unlabeled/`, revision
`307ae50e8e4246de172f195c73e3f026b4f0c7ff`), Copyright The Kubernetes Authors, licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/307ae50e8e4246de172f195c73e3f026b4f0c7ff/LICENSE).

Changes: the editor's metadata (Inkscape and Sodipodi attributes, RDF metadata, ids, unused text styles) is removed;
the drawings are unchanged. `scripts/fetch-kubernetes-icons.mjs` downloads and cleans them again.

Kubernetes is a registered trademark of the Linux Foundation; the icons are shown next to the Kubernetes resources
they stand for.
